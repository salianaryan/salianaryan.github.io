About Me.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Pennsylvania State University interested in how hardware and software work together. I enjoy hands-on projects that let me explore electronics, microcontrollers, sensors, and programming.

I've built small Arduino-based projects, including a motion-detection security system with Wi-Fi alerts and an ultrasonic measurement tool. I'm looking forward to building on that foundation through coursework, personal projects, and the Penn State robotics community. Outside engineering, I compete in shot put and have been involved in student leadership and event coordination.
